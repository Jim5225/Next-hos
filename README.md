## 🌟 Highlights & Features
1. **Authentic Scraped Clinical Data**:
   - Built with exact real-world data from [nexushospitalmymensingh.com](https://nexushospitalmymensingh.com/).
   - Complete directory of **38 specialist doctors** from Mymensingh Medical College & top tertiary hospitals with authentic degrees, designations, photos, visiting hours, and chamber room numbers.
2. **Flagship Cardiac Cath Lab & German Siemens CT Spotlight**:
   - Full Coronary Angiogram (CAG) Complete Package at only **20,000/- BDT**.
   - PTCA Stenting (Heart Ring) and Pacemaker implantation.
   - 24/7 CCU (Pioneer in private CCU in Greater Mymensingh since March 26, 2017).
   - German **SIEMENS** Multi-slice CT Scanner with ongoing **30% Discount**.
   - 24/7 NICU / SCABU for premature and critical newborns (operational since Feb 1, 2021).
3. **AI Doctor Finder & Symptom Matcher**:
   - Intelligent clinical symptom assistant matching patients' complaints to the 38 specialists.
4. **Interactive Digital Health Calculators**:
   - 10-Year Heart Attack & Cardiovascular Risk Estimator.
   - Blood Pressure Category Analyzer.
   - Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculator.
   - Pregnancy Estimated Due Date (EDD).
5. **Interactive Modals**:
   - **Appointment Booking Modal**: Doctor selector, date picker, shift selection, digital token generation, and printable serial card.
   - **Emergency & Ambulance Modal**: 1-tap call to `09611678922` and `01712-421714` (Ambulance), live bed status.
   - **Online Report Download Portal**: Search by Invoice ID (e.g. `NEX-10021`) or phone, interactive report preview with printable PDF view.
   - **Admin / Reception Desk Modal**: Daily serial monitor with PIN protection.
6. **Fully Bilingual**: Seamless switching between Bengali (বাংলা) and English (EN).
